# Copywriting Frameworks

Pick a framework that fits the audience's awareness level.

| Awareness level | What they know | Lead with |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Don't know they have a problem | Story, provocative insight |
| Problem-aware | Feel the pain | Name the pain precisely |
| Solution-aware | Know solutions exist | Your unique mechanism |
| Product-aware | Know you | Proof, comparison, offer |
| Most aware | Ready to buy | Offer + deadline |

## Frameworks

- **AIDA** — Attention, Interest, Desire, Action. Universal default.
- **PAS** — Problem, Agitate, Solution. Best for pain-driven offers and ads.
- **BAB** — Before, After, Bridge. Good for transformations, case studies.
- **4U headlines** — Useful, Urgent, Unique, Ultra-specific. Score each headline 1-4 on each.
- **FAB** — Feature → Advantage → Benefit. Use to translate specs into value.
- **Star-Story-Solution** — character, conflict, resolution with the product.

## Landing page skeleton

1. Hero: headline (outcome + timeframe/specific), subheadline (how), primary CTA, visual
2. Pain / problem section
3. Solution and how it works (3 steps)
4. Benefits (3-6, each with proof)
5. Social proof: logos, reviews, cases with numbers
6. Offer details & pricing, guarantee
7. Objection handling / FAQ
8. Final CTA

## Email campaigns and sequences

> Sequence design, branching rules, templates and benchmarks adapted (modified) from [anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins) `marketing/skills/email-sequence`, Apache-2.0.

**Single email:** subject 30-50 chars (3 variants: curiosity, benefit, urgency/personal); preheader 40-90 chars that adds to, not repeats, the subject; one idea, one primary CTA button; short paragraphs; personalisation tokens where useful.

**Sequence — plan before drafting:** narrative arc across emails, journey stage of each email, how value/urgency escalates, and the conversion that ends the sequence.

For each email give: purpose (one sentence), 2-3 subject lines, preheader, full body, CTA text and link, timing (days after trigger/previous email), who gets or skips it.

**Logic:**
- Branching — e.g. opened but didn't click email 2 → send softer 2b; clicked in email 1 → skip to email 3
- Exit — remove anyone who converts
- Re-entry — whether and when someone can enter again (e.g. win-back after 90 days)
- Suppression — not while in another active sequence, unsubscribed, or just contacted support

**Templates:**
- Onboarding (5-7 emails / 14-21 days): welcome & expectations → quick win → core feature → advanced feature/integration → social proof/community → check-in & feedback → upgrade/next step
- Lead nurture (4-6 / 3-4 weeks): educational value → pain → solution with proof → results/social proof → soft CTA (demo, trial, resource) → direct CTA
- Re-engagement (3-4 / 10-14 days): reason to come back → what they're missing → incentive → last chance
- Win-back (3-5 / 30 days): ask what went wrong → what's new → offer → feedback request → goodbye, door open
- Launch (4-6 / 2-3 weeks): teaser → announcement → feature/use case → early results → limited offer → last reminder
- Event follow-up (3-4 / 7-10 days): thanks + recording → resources → next step offer → survey

**Indicative benchmarks** (vary by industry, list quality, and privacy features that inflate opens):

| Metric | Onboarding | Nurture | Re-engagement | Win-back |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open rate | 50-70% | 20-30% | 15-25% | 15-20% |
| Click rate | 10-20% | 3-7% | 2-5% | 2-4% |
| Conversion | 15-30% | 2-5% | 3-8% | 1-3% |
| Unsubscribe | < 0.5% | < 0.5% | 1-2% | 1-3% |

Deliver: overview table (email | day | goal | subject | CTA), full drafts, a simple flow diagram of branches, A/B test ideas, metrics to track.

## Ad copy checklist

- Matches the targeting segment and the landing page message
- Platform character limits respected
- 3-5 variants with different angles
- No prohibited claims (guaranteed income, medical cures, before/after where banned)
- Ad disclosure / labelling per local law if required
