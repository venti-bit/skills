# Marketing Metrics & Diagnosis

## Core formulas

- CTR = clicks / impressions
- CPC = spend / clicks; CPM = spend / impressions × 1000
- CR (conversion rate) = conversions / visitors (or clicks)
- CPA / CPL = spend / acquisitions (leads)
- ROAS = revenue from ads / ad spend; ROMI = (revenue − marketing cost) / marketing cost
- ER (engagement rate) = (likes + comments + shares + saves) / reach (state the denominator used — by reach or by followers)
- CAC = total sales & marketing cost / new customers; LTV = avg revenue per customer × gross margin × lifetime
- Healthy rule of thumb: LTV / CAC ≥ 3, CAC payback < 12 months (varies by business)

## Funnel diagnosis

Walk the funnel top to bottom and find where the drop is abnormal:

| Symptom | Likely cause | First things to test |
|---|---|---|
| Low CTR, normal CPM | Creative / hook weak or targeting mismatch | New hooks, visuals, audience |
| High CPM | Narrow or competitive audience, low relevance | Broaden audience, refresh creative |
| Good CTR, low CR | Landing page or offer mismatch, slow page | Message match, page speed, form length |
| Leads but no sales | Lead quality or sales process | Qualification, follow-up speed |
| Falling ER | Content fatigue, wrong time, algorithm change | New formats, posting time, interaction prompts |

## A/B testing

- Change one variable at a time; define the primary metric beforehand.
- Need enough volume: as a rough guide, at least ~100 conversions per variant for reliable conclusions; otherwise call results "directional".
- Run for full weekly cycles (7/14 days) to avoid day-of-week bias.
- Report: hypothesis, variants, sample sizes, result with confidence, decision.

## Report structure

1. Summary: 3 bullet conclusions + recommended actions
2. KPI table vs. previous period and vs. target
3. What worked / what didn't, with evidence
4. Next steps with expected impact
