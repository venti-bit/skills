# People Management

## Onboarding plan (30-60-90)
| Period | Goals | Activities | Owner | Success check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before day 1 | Accounts, equipment, welcome message | | HR/IT | Ready on day 1 |
| Week 1 | Context, people, tools | Intro meetings, buddy, docs | Manager | First small task done |
| 30 days | Learn | Shadowing, first deliverables | | Check-in meeting |
| 60 days | Contribute | Own tasks independently | | Feedback session |
| 90 days | Own | Full scope, probation review | | Probation decision |

## Feedback models
- **SBI**: Situation → Behaviour → Impact, then ask for their view and agree on next steps
- Be specific and timely; separate behaviour from personality; balance is not about "sandwiching" but about accuracy
- For difficult conversations, prepare: goal, facts, expected reactions, desired outcome, follow-up date

## Performance review
- Inputs: goals/OKRs achieved, competency evidence, peer/360 feedback, self-review
- Rate goals and behaviours separately; avoid recency bias (use notes from the whole period)
- Output: strengths with examples, growth areas with examples, development plan (2-3 actions), rating with justification
- Performance improvement plan (PIP): specific gaps, measurable expectations, support offered, timeline, check-in schedule — check local law before linking to termination

## Competency matrix
Rows: competencies (hard + soft). Columns: levels (e.g. Junior / Middle / Senior / Lead). Each cell: observable behaviours, not adjectives ("independently designs services handling X load" vs. "good at architecture").

## Employee surveys (eNPS, engagement)
- eNPS = % promoters (9-10) − % detractors (0-6) on "How likely are you to recommend us as a place to work?"
- Keep surveys anonymous, short (≤ 15 questions), with 1-2 open questions; report only for groups ≥ 5 people
- Close the loop: share results and 2-3 actions taken

## Policies
Purpose, scope, rules, responsibilities, exceptions, effective date, owner. Flag items regulated by labour law (working hours, leave, remote work, termination, disciplinary procedures, personal data) for legal review.
