# Hiring Playbook

## 1. Job description
Structure:
1. Title (market-standard, searchable) + level + format (office/hybrid/remote, location) + salary range if possible
2. About the company / team (2-3 sentences, specific)
3. Mission of the role: what success looks like in 6-12 months
4. Responsibilities (5-8 bullets, outcome-oriented)
5. Must-have requirements (≤ 6) — each must be truly necessary
6. Nice-to-have (≤ 4)
7. What we offer (concrete: salary, equipment, learning budget, schedule — not "friendly team")
8. Hiring process steps and timeline
Use inclusive, gender-neutral language; avoid "rockstar/ninja", unnecessary years-of-experience demands, and age-coded wording ("young team", "digital native").

## 2. Sourcing & outreach
- Channels: job boards, LinkedIn/HH-type platforms, communities, referrals, GitHub/Behance for portfolios
- Boolean search template: `("backend" OR "back-end") AND (Go OR Golang) AND (Kubernetes OR k8s) NOT (junior OR intern)`
- Outreach message: personalised hook (their work) → role in one sentence → why it's interesting for *them* → salary range → easy CTA (15-min call). Under 120 words. Follow up once after 3-5 days.

## 3. Structured interview
- Define 4-6 competencies from the role mission
- For each: 2 behavioural questions ("Tell me about a time…", STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result) and/or situational ones ("What would you do if…")
- Anchored rating scale per competency (1 = no evidence, 3 = meets bar, 5 = exceptional, with descriptions)
- Same questions for all candidates; interviewers score independently before discussing

Scorecard:

| Competency | Question(s) | Evidence heard | Rating 1-5 | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 4. Test assignments
- Must mirror real work, take ≤ 2-4 hours, have clear evaluation criteria shared up-front
- Don't request work the company will use for free; consider paid assignments for long ones
- Provide: context, task, deliverables, deadline, evaluation criteria, contact for questions

## 5. Decision & offer
- Debrief: compare scorecards per competency, not overall impressions
- Reference checks: ask about specific behaviours and examples
- Offer letter: title, start date, compensation (gross/net per local practice), bonus, benefits, probation, working format, acceptance deadline. Final employment contract must comply with local labour law.
