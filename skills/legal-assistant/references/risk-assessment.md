# Legal Risk Assessment (Severity × Likelihood)

> Adapted (modified) from [anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins) `legal/skills/legal-risk-assessment`, Apache-2.0. Thresholds are a starting point — adapt to the user's risk appetite.

## Scales
**Severity** (if the risk materialises), relative to the deal / contract value or the business:
1 Negligible — no material impact · 2 Low — < 1% exposure, no publicity · 3 Moderate — 1-5%, noticeable disruption · 4 High — 5-25%, significant disruption, regulator interest likely · 5 Critical — > 25%, business-threatening, regulatory action or personal liability of directors

**Likelihood:** 1 Remote · 2 Unlikely · 3 Possible (foreseeable trigger) · 4 Likely (common trigger) · 5 Almost certain (trigger present)

**Score = Severity × Likelihood**

| Score | Level | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1-4 | GREEN low | Accept, record, review periodically |
| 5-9 | YELLOW medium | Mitigate or negotiate, assign an owner, define triggers that would escalate |
| 10-15 | ORANGE high | Escalate to senior lawyer / management, written mitigation and contingency plan, consider outside counsel |
| 16-25 | RED critical | Immediate escalation to leadership, outside counsel, preserve evidence (litigation hold), notify insurers / regulators if required |

## Risk memo
```
## Legal Risk Assessment — [matter]
Date · Prepared by · Confidential / privileged: yes/no

1. Risk description
2. Facts and context
3. Severity [1-5] — rationale (exposure, operations, reputation)
   Likelihood [1-5] — rationale (precedent, triggers)
   Score: [n] — [GREEN/YELLOW/ORANGE/RED]
4. Aggravating factors
5. Mitigating factors
6. Options
   | Option | Effectiveness | Cost / effort | Recommended |
7. Recommendation
8. Residual risk after mitigation
9. Monitoring and escalation triggers
```
Use this matrix to justify the High/Med/Low labels in contract reviews when the stakes are high or the user needs to report the risk to management.
